Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

The short version. Trail has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show them. Trail Plus removes all ads.

This policy explains what information the Trail mobile app (“Trail”, “the app”, “we”) handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the iOS and Android versions of the app.

What stays on your device

Trail stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us, and we have no way to see it:

  • your puzzle progress, solve times, streak and statistics;
  • your settings, such as theme and reminder time;
  • whether you own Trail Plus (a cached copy, re-checked with the App Store or Google Play).

Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run analytics, we do not operate servers that receive data from the app, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Trail shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK inside the app may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (the IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the Advertising ID on Android);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices about ads

  • iPhone tracking permission. After your first solve, iOS may ask whether Trail can track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, Google does not receive your IDFA and ads are less personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. You can review or withdraw consent at any time from the app’s Settings screen.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device’s Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Trail Plus. Buying Trail Plus turns off all ads. With no ads, the AdMob SDK requests nothing.

Purchases

Trail Plus is sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple or Google processes your payment under their own privacy policies. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only learns whether the purchase exists, so it can unlock Plus.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device. No push service and no server are involved, and you can turn it off in the app or in your device settings.

Children

Trail is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data, most requests are best made to Google for advertising data, or handled by deleting the app. We are glad to help with any request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

Contact
