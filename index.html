One line.
Every square.

Trail is a daily path puzzle for iOS and Android. Draw a single line with your thumb that fills the whole board and touches the numbered checkpoints in order.

A new puzzle every day, the same one for everyone.

300 levels across ten chapters, from gentle to severe.

Works offline. No account, no sign-in.

Support

Questions, bugs or a puzzle that seems impossible? Open an issue at github.com/alisherrahimov/trail/issues.
